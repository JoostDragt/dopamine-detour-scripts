## ⏱️ How the timing works (read this first)

**45 images** across ~4:40 of voiceover = **a new picture every ~6 seconds** (always inside the
5–10 s window — nothing static for longer). The **Start** column is on the **voiceover timeline**
(the 8 Mark clips laid end-to-end). The finished video runs ~7–9 min once beats are held + music added.

> ⚠️ **Precision caveat:** these seconds are **computed** from Episode 1's *measured* speaking rate
> (Mark ≈ 156 wpm), because this environment's egress policy blocks Higgsfield's audio CDN, so the real
> clip lengths can't be read here. Expect **±1–2 s** drift. **To lock it exactly:** read the 8 clip
> durations in Higgsfield and nudge, **or** cut on the **VO cue** words against the waveform — that is
> frame-accurate regardless of the estimate. The cue column is the source of truth; the seconds are a guide.

**Shot type:** ⭐ = hero scene (full composition) · • = connective beat (simpler, keeps the pace up).
