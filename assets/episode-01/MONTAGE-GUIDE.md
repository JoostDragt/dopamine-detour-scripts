## ✂️ MONTAGE GUIDE — assemble at 12 fps

1. **New project at 12 fps**, 1920×1080 (16:9). Set your sequence frame rate to **12**.
2. **Drop the 7 VO files end-to-end** on the audio track in order (vo-1 → vo-7). They form the spine (~4:43). Add ~0.5–1s of silence between sections where the script "breathes" (after the hook, before the final two lines).
3. **Lay each scene image over its matching VO** (see the table — the "VO" column tells you which section each scene sits under). Roughly:
   - VO1 → scenes 1,2,3 · VO2 → 4,5,6,7 · VO3 → 8,9 · VO4 → 10,11,12,13 · VO5 → 14,15,16,17 · VO6 → 18,19 · VO7 → 20.
   - For scene 18 (4-panel montage) cut between the panels, or push in on each quarter.
4. **Add motion in the editor (this is your "animation"):** slow push-ins, slides, and the whiteboard "draw-on" reveal. Keep moves simple. Because the project is 12 fps, everything keeps the hand-drawn stutter. **Turn OFF frame-blending / optical-flow.**
5. **Burn in the on-screen text** (hand-marker font) from the table, key words in yellow. Turn on full captions.
6. **Music + SFX:** light warm marimba/ukulele bed; drop to near-silence under "Your brain isn't broken" (scene 2) and the final lines (scene 20). Add marker "scribble" SFX on each draw-on, soft pops on the tabs.
7. **Export** at 12 fps, H.264, 1080p.
8. **Thumbnail:** A/B test A vs. B; C is the on-brand fallback.
